DK Football Moonshot — implemented behavior

Positive rush/completed pass: +0.2x; every full 10 yards on that play: +0.5x; credited first down: +0.5x; offensive TD: +2.0x. Action values double on the 10th qualifying rush/completed pass and every fifth thereafter, and beginning with the second scoring event; bonus factors compound.

An incomplete pass or offensive turnover ends an active round and loses an unresolved wager. Before an eligible round start, a designated failure voids it. At end of half/game, unresolved active wagers settle at the current multiplier, even below 1x. A winning payout uses the selected target, not the amount by which the event exceeded it.

Manual interpretation to verify against DK settlement receipts: the 10th and 15th qualifying action and second scoring event receive their newly activated bonus immediately. Positive pass, rushing play, scoring-event counts and unusual penalty cases must be validated against actual settlements before model admission. The tracker stops at wager settlement, not at the next DK round boundary.

SportsEdge gating: all retrospective rows are PAPER / 0u. No sportsbook scraping, automatic bets, live prices, team-specific calibrated probabilities, edge stars or claims of +EV. To move this lane forward: capture DK quoted target(s) before each round, immutable play-by-play with source timestamps, settlement receipts, forward walk testing, confidence intervals, and calibration against the market.
