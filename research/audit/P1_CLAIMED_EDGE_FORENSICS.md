# P1.1 Claimed-edge forensics — research only (PAPER/0u)

`pregame_edge_audit.py` historically ranked only structurally valid candidates. That makes it inappropriate as a complete adversarial largest-edge screen: flagged longshots, late snapshots and missing original hashes could vanish from the top-N. This draft does not alter that original ranking, but exposes **the entire computable claimed-edge ranking** including invalid records and a second game/market/direction cluster view. Selection and quote times are checked as declared, not independently authenticated. The original kickoff-relative 120-minute lookback is clearly labeled **research only**; the 300-second selected-vs-captured admission check is separate. All output is PAPER/0u, `source_authenticated=false`, `admission_allowed=false`.

The cluster view picks the highest claimed EV rung as a concentration representative, **not** an independent betting trial or a main-line estimate. All original rows remain in the uncollapsed claimed ranking and flagged list. No outcome-dependent filtering, edits to original historical data, calibrated probabilities, release claim, or change to frozen v5.
